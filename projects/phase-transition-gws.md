title: Gravitational waves from phase transitions in the early Universe
level: [phd, honours, other]

prerequisites: Thermodynamics and quantum mechanics; Python. General relativity helpful.

If the electroweak symmetry of the early Universe broke through a first-order
phase transition, expanding bubbles of the new phase would have collided and
produced a stochastic background of gravitational waves. Future detectors
such as LISA could observe this background.

You will calculate the phase transition and its gravitational-wave spectrum
in extensions of the Standard Model, and find out which models future
observatories could test. A shorter version of this project is suitable for a
summer research scholarship.
