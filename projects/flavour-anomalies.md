prerequisites: Quantum mechanics; particle physics at third-year level.
---

Several measurements of *b*-hadron decays have shown tensions with Standard
Model predictions. In this project you will use effective field theory to work
out which new particles could explain the data. You will then look at how
these explanations can be confirmed or ruled out by upcoming measurements at
LHCb and Belle II.
