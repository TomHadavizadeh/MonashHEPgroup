When quarks and gluons fly apart after a collision, the strong force between
them is thought to form "strings" of colour field that break into hadrons.
In dense environments, such as high-multiplicity proton–proton collisions, these
strings can overlap and interact. This produces effects once thought to be unique
to heavy-ion collisions.

You will develop and test models of string interactions in the PYTHIA event
generator and compare them with LHC measurements, contributing to one of the most
widely used programs in particle physics.
