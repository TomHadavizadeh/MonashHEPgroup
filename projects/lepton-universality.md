title: Testing lepton universality in b-hadron decays
level: [phd]
areas: [lhcb, flavour]
prerequisites: Particle physics at third-year level; Python or C++.
---

In the Standard Model, electrons, muons and taus interact identically apart
from their different masses. You will measure ratios of *b*-hadron decay
rates to different leptons using LHCb data, one of the cleanest tests of this
"lepton universality". The project works closely with the Monash flavour
theory group.
