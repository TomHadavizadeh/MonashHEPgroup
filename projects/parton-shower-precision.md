Parton showers simulate the cascade of radiation from high-energy quarks and
gluons. Improving their formal accuracy is essential for the precision physics
programme of the High-Luminosity LHC and future colliders.

This project extends the VINCIA antenna shower in PYTHIA with higher-order
corrections, and validates the results against analytic calculations and data.
