title: Trigger development for the COMET experiment
level: [phd, honours, other]
areas: [comet, detector-development]
prerequisites: Interest in electronics and programming. Experience with FPGAs or C++ is a plus.
---

COMET must find a single signal electron among an enormous rate of
background hits. You will help develop the trigger system that makes this
selection, working on firmware, simulation studies or test-stand measurements,
depending on your interests. Shorter versions are available as summer projects.
