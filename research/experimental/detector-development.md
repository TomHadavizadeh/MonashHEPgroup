Every new measurement in particle physics starts with an instrument that can
make it. The Monash group designs, builds and tests detector systems for the
experiments we work on, in laboratories on the Clayton campus and at partner
institutes overseas.

## Current work

- **Trigger electronics for COMET.** Readout and trigger boards that pick out
  candidate signal electrons from a very high-rate muon beam environment.
- **Timing detectors.** Fast scintillator and Cherenkov counters that measure
  when a particle passed through, to a fraction of a nanosecond.
- **LHCb upgrades.** Research and development for future upgrades of the
  LHCb detector, which must cope with much higher collision rates at the
  High-Luminosity LHC.

## Skills you will learn

Projects in detector development combine physics with electronics, firmware
(FPGA) programming, mechanical design and data analysis. They suit students who
enjoy working in the lab as well as at a computer.
