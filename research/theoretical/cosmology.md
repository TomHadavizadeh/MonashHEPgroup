The Standard Model cannot explain most of what the Universe is made of. It has
no particle that could be dark matter, no explanation for why there is more
matter than antimatter, and nothing to say about dark energy. Particle
cosmology looks for the physics that fills these gaps.

## Our research

- **Dark matter.** Its properties and how it might be detected, from direct
  detection experiments to the LHC.
- **Phase transitions and gravitational waves.** Symmetry breaking in the
  early Universe may have happened through violent first-order phase
  transitions. These would leave a background of gravitational waves that future
  detectors could observe.
- **Baryogenesis.** The origin of the cosmic matter–antimatter asymmetry.
- **Inflation and dark energy.** Models of the very early and very late Universe.

## Global fits

Monash is part of **GAMBIT** (the Global And Modular Beyond-the-Standard-Model
Inference Tool). GAMBIT combines data from colliders, astrophysics, cosmology
and precision experiments to work out which theories of new physics are
still viable.
