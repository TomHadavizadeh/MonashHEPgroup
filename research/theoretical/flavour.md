The Standard Model contains three copies, or *generations*, of each type of
quark and lepton. They are identical except for their masses and the way they mix
with one another. Nothing in the theory explains this pattern. The
"flavour puzzle" is one of the clearest signs that a deeper theory is waiting
to be found.

## Our research

- **The origin of flavour.** We build models that explain the observed masses
  and mixings, and connect flavour to the grand unification of forces.
- **Flavour anomalies.** Several measurements of *b*-hadron decays have shown
  tensions with Standard Model predictions. We study which new particles
  could explain them and how future data can test those explanations.
- **Effective field theories.** We use effective field theory, chiral
  perturbation theory and heavy-quark methods to make precise predictions for
  hadronic processes.
- **New physics in the scalar sector.** We study extended Higgs sectors and new
  coloured scalars, and their signatures at colliders and in rare decays.

This work links directly to the group's experiments: LHCb measures *b*- and
*c*-hadron decays, and COMET searches for lepton flavour violation.
