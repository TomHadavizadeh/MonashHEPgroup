title: Research projects
---

Projects currently offered by the group. If one interests you, email the
supervisor to talk it through. Most projects can be adjusted to fit your
background, and many come in PhD, Honours and shorter versions. You are also
welcome to propose your own idea.

For entry requirements and scholarships, see the School of Physics and
Astronomy's [postgraduate pages](https://www.monash.edu/science/schools/physics/postgrad).
