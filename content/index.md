---
heading: Monash Particle Physics
---

We are the particle physics group in the School of Physics and Astronomy at
Monash University. We study the smallest building blocks of nature and the
forces between them.

Our theorists work on the strong force, the puzzle of flavour, and the
particle physics of the early Universe. Our experimentalists search for new
physics at CERN's Large Hadron Collider and at the COMET experiment in Japan,
and build the detectors those searches need. The two halves of the group work
closely together.
